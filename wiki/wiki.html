Cape is a cosmetic Minecraft mod that allows you to change your in-game cape to any of the 50+ options, offered by vanilla!

Features

  • 50+ Cape Designs: Choose from a wide selection of cape designs found in vanilla Minecraft.
  • In-Game Selection: Easily choose your preferred cape through Cape's selection interface.
  • Persistent Preferences: Your selected cape is saved between game sessions.
  • Vanilla Designs: Enjoy Minecraft's existing cape designs through a dedicated cosmetic mod.
Credits Cape is an unofficial community project and is not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft. All original cape designs remain the property of their respective owners.
